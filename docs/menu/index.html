Menu

Our menu is built around handcrafted sweets, specialty soft serve, and colorful drinks made to feel both nostalgic and new. Our menu blends Japanese-inspired dessert traditions with a playful fantasy café experience, approachable enough for anyone to enjoy, but layered with small magical details for guests who want to discover more.
