Event Policies & Booking Guidelines

Lead Time & Scheduling

All reservations must be scheduled with our team at least 1 week (7 days) in advance to ensure staffing and inventory readiness.

Deposit & Credit Application

The $100 deposit for Large Groups and Private Buyouts secures your date/time, and is applied directly toward your event's final food and drink tab.

Menu Pre-Orders

Pre-ordering items is required for private buyouts (72 hours notice) and highly encouraged for large tables to maintain kitchen pacing and fresh service.

Gratuity & Service Standards

An automatic 18% service gratuity is applied to group tabs of 10 or more guests to support our dedicated Guild team members.

Large Group Inquiries (30+ Guests): For parties over 30, please call our store or email us at wandandwhiskcafe@gmail.com to hear adjusted rates and custom arrangements for parties that large.

Ready to book your gathering? Speak with our café management team or submit an inquiry to reserve your date!
