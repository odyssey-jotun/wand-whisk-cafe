Event Policies & Booking Guidelines

Lead Time & Scheduling

All reservations must be scheduled with our team at least 1 week (7 days) in advance to ensure staffing and inventory readiness.

Deposit & Credit Application

The $100 deposit for Large Groups and Private Buyouts secures your date/time, and is applied directly toward your event's final food and drink tab.

Menu Pre-Orders

Pre-ordering items is required for private buyouts (72 hours notice) and highly encouraged for large tables to maintain kitchen pacing and fresh service.

Gratuity & Service Standards

An automatic 18% service gratuity is applied to group tabs of 10 or more guests to support our dedicated Guild team members.

Large Group Inquiries (30+ Guests): For parties over 30, please call our store or email us at wandandwhiskcafe@gmail.com to hear adjusted rates and custom arrangements for parties that large.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wand & Whisk Café?

Wand & Whisk Café is a Japanese-inspired dessert and luxury ice cream café in Little Rock, Arkansas, built for fans of anime, tabletop games, trading cards, fantasy, and video games. It serves taiyaki, rolled Japanese crepes, soufflé pancakes, and potion sodas.

Where is Wand & Whisk Café located?

We’re located in the Outlets of Little Rock in Little Rock, Arkansas. You’ll find us across from Otaku Takeout and the Lucky Cat Arcade. We’re also right next to The Dojo, a TTRPG gaming and trading card store. If you’re trying to get directions for your GPS, though, here’s our address:

11201 Bass Pro Pkwy Suite M147
Little Rock, AR 72210

What are the hours of the Wand and Whisk Café?

Our normal business operation hours are as follows:

Monday–Saturday: 11:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.

Sunday: 11:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.

What makes your ice cream different from regular soft serve?

Our soft serve is real milk cream at 12.5% milk fat, which meets the USDA standard for actual ice cream and runs about double what you’ll find elsewhere in Arkansas. It’s crafted in-house under our Head Chef and benchmarked against Japan’s luxury soft serve tradition.

What is taiyaki?

A warm, fish-shaped pastry cone, filled with custard or Nutella and topped with our in-house soft serve.

What’s on the menu?

Japanese-inspired crepes, taiyaki soft serve, soufflé pancakes, and potion sodas, plus seasonal creations from the Dessert Guild.

Do you host birthday parties or group events?

Yes. The café is designed for celebrating the things you love, so birthdays and group meetups are a natural fit. Watch the Events section for tastings, themed experiences, and seasonal drops.

Are you kid-friendly / family-friendly?

Yes. The menu and space are approachable for anyone, with magical details layered in for guests who want to discover more.
